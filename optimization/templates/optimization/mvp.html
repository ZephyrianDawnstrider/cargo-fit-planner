Supported dry-container examples

20 ft standard, 40 ft standard and 40 ft high cube dry steel examples. Custom measured dry closed box is unverified.

Road chassis carrying a freight container

The same container geometry can be assessed; the chassis, axles, road limits and vehicle/load securement are not modeled. Treat road transport as a separate manual hold.

Container profiles · specification only

45 ft high cube, ventilated, reefer, open-top and hardtop containers are not accepted by this dry-box calculation; their distinct profiles and operating constraints remain unmodeled.

Road freight bodies · manual planning hold

Dry-box and curtain-side trailers, swap bodies, and freight containers on road chassis require separate opening, axle, floor, support and securement rules. The container-on-chassis case uses container geometry only; road transport stays on hold.

Specialized equipment · manual planning hold

Flat-rack/platform and paper or coil equipment require support-point, centre-of-gravity, overhang and securing rules that are not modeled.

Tank equipment · out of scope for box packing

Tank containers need separate volumetric and liquid-load methods; a rectangular box fit does not represent their cargo.

Equipment-family references: Maersk container families · Hapag-Lloyd type groups · KRONE trailer and swap-system families.

Modeled scope and limits
  • One selected dry freight-container profile; published dimensions/payload are examples, not guaranteed equipment capacities.
  • Gross load-unit bounding boxes, axis-aligned placement, and fixed or yaw orientation.
  • “Can support cargo above” and “floor only” are separate simplified placement constraints, not strength or bearing approval.
  • Dangerous goods are withheld for manual compliance review; no IMDG segregation, stowage, booking or transport approval is performed.
  • No loading order, lashing, securing, axle limits, point-load limits, overhang or cargo strength analysis.
