1. Enter cargo

Add gross load units one per row, or paste tab-separated rows copied from Excel. Dimensions convert to whole millimetres; gross weight stays in kilograms at 0.001 kg precision. Palletized dimensions and weight include the pallet. “Floor only” prevents stacking this unit above cargo; “can support cargo above” is permission only. A positive rated top-load limit and rating source are also required before any cargo can be stacked on a unit. Blank means unknown, so nothing is placed above it. Up to 100 expanded units per pack.

Handling update: older manifests still load, but “stackable” alone no longer permits a stack. Enter the package’s reported maximum cargo mass above it and its source; blank/unknown capacity means no cargo above. The limit covers every unit above through the support chain, not just the next box. Only one full-footprint supporting unit is modeled; no bridging, tilt, tipping, shared supports or dynamic strength approval.

20 ft standard, 40 ft standard and 40 ft high cube dry steel examples. Custom measured dry closed box is unverified.

Road chassis carrying a freight container

The same container geometry can be assessed; the chassis, axles, road limits and vehicle/load securement are not modeled. Treat road transport as a separate manual hold.

Container profiles · specification only

45 ft high cube, ventilated, reefer, open-top and hardtop containers are not accepted by this dry-box calculation; their distinct profiles and operating constraints remain unmodeled.

Road freight bodies · manual planning hold

Dry-box and curtain-side trailers, swap bodies, and freight containers on road chassis require separate opening, axle, floor, support and securement rules. The container-on-chassis case uses container geometry only; road transport stays on hold.

Specialized equipment · manual planning hold

Flat-rack/platform and paper or coil equipment require support-point, centre-of-gravity, overhang and securing rules that are not modeled.

Tank equipment · out of scope for box packing

Tank containers need separate volumetric and liquid-load methods; a rectangular box fit does not represent their cargo.

Equipment-family references: Maersk container families · Hapag-Lloyd type groups · KRONE trailer and swap-system families.

Shipment and document references

Optional references are carried into exports; they do not reserve equipment or create a booking or bill of lading.

Editable freight quote (optional)

No universal freight average is assumed. Enter a dated carrier quote for this route and equipment. Unmatched or expired quotes remain unpriced, never free. This estimate applies to placed cargo only; it does not affect geometric packing.

Quote origin and destination must match Shipment and document references. Values are kept only in the submitted snapshot and JSON export. CSV cost columns repeat the whole-load estimate on each row: do not sum them.

Measured tolerance and planning clearances

Optional user-specified allowances, not international minimums or automatic humidity/temperature predictions. Positive allowances conservatively disable all stacking and cannot yet be combined with customer allocations. Enter measured worst-case deviations or approved handling instructions; verify loading access separately.

Tolerance is added once to each total L/W/H, not per face. No gap is added underneath cargo. Door deduction is subtracted once from total opening width/height. Zero means no allowance modeled, not safe tight packing. Software input range 0–100 mm is not a recommended range. No refrigerated-airflow or DG segregation check.

Customer allocation and reserved regions

Assign every source Item ID (before quantity expansion) to exactly one customer. Weight and volume caps are separate percentages of the full equipment capacity; each total must be at most 100%. A 50% quota alone does not reserve half the floor.

Choose shared space for every customer, or disjoint physical regions for every customer. X runs from the closed end (0) to the door; Y runs across width from the origin; Z runs from floor upwards. Segment numbers start at 1. Elevated Z regions are held without verified support. No partition wall, load strength or access path is inferred.

Customer reservations cannot yet be combined with positive planning allowances. Dunnage, air circulation and loading sequence are not modeled. No universal safety gap is assumed. Do not treat a geometrically touching fit as dispatch approval.
