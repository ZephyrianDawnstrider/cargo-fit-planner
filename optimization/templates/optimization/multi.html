Plan successive containers

One manifest. One selected dry-box profile. Up to five successive loads.

A bounded sequential heuristic, not minimum-container or minimum-cost optimization. Special-handling cargo stays on manual review hold. No booking, dispatch approval, loading sequence or physical transport certification is produced.

1. Enter the manifest and limits

The initial manifest is synthetic sample cargo. Paste a CSV using the existing manifest template. Maximum 256 KiB, 100 source rows and 100 expanded units across the entire plan. Nothing is saved to an application database. No tracking or third-party lookup is triggered here.

Measured custom profile and optional allowances

Leave both blank for published geometry without additional allowances. Customer allocations and manual placement snapshots are not supported in this mode. Positive allowances retain the existing floor-only rules. Unknown measurements must not be invented.

Schema: name; inside_mm with length/width/height; door_mm with width/height; max_payload_kg; dimensions_source with kind (user_measurement, equipment_plate, or carrier_document) and reference. Use whole millimetres and gross payload kilograms. All fields are required for a custom profile.

Allowed keys: dimension_tolerance_mm, horizontal_gap_mm, wall_clearance_mm, roof_clearance_mm, door_clearance_mm, source_reference. Values are whole millimetres 0–100; positive values require a source. These are software bounds, not recommended physical clearances.
